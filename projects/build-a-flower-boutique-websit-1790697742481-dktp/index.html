Expert Advice

Floral Care & Preservation Guide

Keep your blossoms vibrant and flourishing with these professional florist tips.

Trimming Stems Properly

Trim 1 to 2 inches off your flower stems at a 45-degree angle before placing them in water. This increases water absorption surface area and prevents stems from resting flat on the bottom of the vase.

Water & Flower Food Maintenance

Use clean lukewarm water and mix in the provided floral packet. Change the water and re-trim stems every 2 to 3 days to eliminate bacteria buildup.

Ideal Placement Environment

Display your arrangement away from direct sunlight, heating vents, ceiling fans, and ripening fruits which release ethylene gas that speeds up aging.
